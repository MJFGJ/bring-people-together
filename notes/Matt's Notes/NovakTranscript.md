# Interview: Novak

**Length:** about 20 minutes (small talk cut)
**Raw transcript:** [NovakTranscript-raw.md](NovakTranscript-raw.md)

## Key points

- **Daily life:** gym in the morning, classes, works and eats lunch at the Chalmers Cafe, then studio. Tries to stay off his phone in downtime.
- **Social style:** extroverted. Prefers seeing people *outside* the home, in new settings.
- **Groups:** freshman dorm floor (fantasy football group chat, beer Olympics), intramural basketball, family group chat. Has lost touch with most friend group chats from home.
- **How groups formed:** almost entirely proximity (dorm floor, who he sat with). Doesn't actively seek out new people.
- **What makes hangouts fun:** seeing people relax and show different sides of themselves, and seeing people be passionate about something.
- **What keeps him coming back:** team camaraderie (nostalgic from growing up in sports) and making shared memories and inside jokes. Not about winning.
- **Wishes he did more of:** reading, but as something shared rather than a book club. He likes sharing big moments in a story, like watching Lord of the Rings or Game of Thrones together, or reading side by side on the beach.
- **Barriers:** schoolwork and the anxiety about it. FOMO doesn't affect him much.
- **What might help:** seeing other people's progress motivates him. A shared checklist or accountability (seeing that someone finished their tasks, not their actual work) would push him.
